**Project description** I am a Data-Driven Life Science postdoc. I am currently developing and applying AI-based methods to understand floral colour loss on oceanic islands. To do so, I apply AI approaches together with macro-evolutionary and phylogenetic models. One of the goals of my fellowship is to help use natural history collection data to better understand traits and biodiversity loss.
