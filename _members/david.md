I am a Postdoc at the Swedish Museum of Natural History.
<br>
**Taxa**
_Tribulus_
<br>
**Project description** I am working with Ancestral Recombination Graphs, trying to understand whether the genus Tribulus, one of the favorite plants of Darwin's finches, colonized the Galápagos before or after human presence. I am also focusing on the Scalesia radiation, trying to understand whether we can detect the colonization bottleneck of the islands.
<br>
**Project collaborators** Felipe Zapata (UCLA), Elisa Bonaccorso (Universidad San Francisco de Quito), Sofia Carvajal (Universidad San Francisco de Quito), Daniel Reyes-Corral (McGill University).
