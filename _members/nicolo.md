I am a Postdoc at the University of Oslo, part of IslandParadox.
<br>
**Taxa**
Galápagos Daisies
<br>
**Project description** I am focusing on genome evolution in Galápagos daisies. In my project I am applying pangenome approaches to understand if gene presence absence variation affects diversification, improving ARG inference approaches and understanding the evolution of plant centromeres.
<br>
**Project collaborators** ‪Detlef Weigel‬ (Max Planck Institute for Biology Tübingen), Loren Rieseberg (University of British Columbia), Ole Tørresen (University of Oslo), Kjetill Jakobsen (University of Oslo)
